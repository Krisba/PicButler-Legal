What is PicButler?

PicButler is an iPhone app for cleaning up and organizing your photo library. It groups duplicate and similar photos, picks the best shot in each group by sharpness, lighting and resolution, and explains why it won. Then it helps you keep only what matters from each trip or event and saves it as an album in Apple Photos. Photo analysis runs on your iPhone.

How it works

  1. It finds your moments

    PicButler goes through your library month by month and spots your trips, nights out and holidays. Each one becomes a chapter you can finish in a few minutes. Prefer to start with this week? You can.

  2. It clears the duplicates first

    Exact copies and near-identical shots are grouped together. PicButler keeps the best one in each group and tells you why it won. Prefer a different photo? Tap to keep it instead.

  3. You swipe through the rest

    Swipe to keep or let go of each remaining photo. Pick your favorites and PicButler gets them ready to share on Instagram.

  4. Your album lands in Apple Photos

    The photos you keep are saved as an album in the Photos app, named after the moment, so it syncs to your other devices with iCloud Photos. Everything you let go of goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is PicButler free?

Scanning your library, clearing screenshots and finishing your first album are free. Everything else needs PicButler Pro: $6.99 a month, or $29.99 a year with a 3-day free trial. You can cancel anytime from the App Store.

Does PicButler upload my photos?

No. PicButler analyzes your photos on your iPhone. Your photos never leave the device, and the only permission it asks for is access to your photo library.

How is PicButler different from the Duplicates album in Apple Photos?

Apple's Duplicates collection merges copies of the same photo. PicButler also groups photos that are similar but not identical, like several takes of the same pose, picks the best one by sharpness, lighting and resolution, and tells you why it won. Read the full comparison.

What happens to the photos I delete?

They go to the Recently Deleted album in Apple Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. PicButler never deletes anything without your confirmation.

Which iPhones does PicButler work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
