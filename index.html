What is PicButler?

PicButler is your personal photo butler for iPhone. It frees up storage by going through your camera roll with you, month by month: it clears the clutter, helps you keep only the photos you love from every trip and event, and saves them as albums in Apple Photos. Everything stays on your phone.

How it works

  1. It finds your moments

    PicButler goes through your library month by month and spots your trips, nights out and holidays. Each one becomes a chapter you can finish in a few minutes. Prefer to start with this week? You can.

  2. It clears the duplicates first

    Exact copies and near-identical shots are grouped together. PicButler keeps the best one in each group and tells you why it won. Prefer a different photo? Tap to keep it instead.

  3. You swipe through the rest

    Swipe to keep or let go of each remaining photo. Pick your favorites and PicButler gets them ready to share on Instagram.

  4. Your album lands in Apple Photos

    The photos you keep are saved as an album in the Photos app, named after the moment, so it syncs to your other devices with iCloud Photos. Everything you let go of goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Rediscover, every day

Every day, Rediscover deals you ten photos of one theme from across your years. Keep the ones you love and they go to that theme's album in Apple Photos. Free for everyone.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

  • PicButler analyzes your photos on your device. They never leave it.
  • It asks only for your photo library, plus notifications if you want them. No contacts, microphone, calendar or location.
  • No ads, ever. No tracking across other apps, and no "Allow tracking?" prompt.
  • No AI slop: PicButler never generates or retouches your photos. Every pick comes with its reason, and you make the call.
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation, and deleted photos can be recovered for 30 days.

Frequently asked questions

Is PicButler free?

You can curate any month of your photos for free, clear your old screenshots, and use Rediscover every day. The rest of your library needs PicButler Pro: $6.99 a month, or $29.99 a year, billed once. You can cancel anytime from the App Store.

Does PicButler upload my photos?

No. PicButler analyzes your photos on your iPhone and they never leave it. It asks only for access to your photo library, plus notifications if you want them.

How is PicButler different from the Duplicates album in Apple Photos?

Apple's Duplicates collection merges copies of the same photo. PicButler also groups photos that are similar but not identical, like several takes of the same pose, picks the best one by sharpness, lighting and resolution, and tells you why it won. Read the full comparison.

Does PicButler use AI?

It uses Apple's on-device image analysis to group similar photos and to score sharpness, lighting and faces. It never generates or retouches your photos, it shows why it picked each one, and you always make the final call.

What happens to the photos I delete?

They go to the Recently Deleted album in Apple Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. PicButler never deletes anything without your confirmation.

Which iPhones does PicButler work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
