PicButler press kit

Free up space. Keep what matters.

What it is

PicButler is your personal photo butler for iPhone. It frees up storage by going through your camera roll with you, month by month: it clears the clutter, helps you keep only the photos you love from every trip and event, and saves them as albums in Apple Photos. Everything stays on your phone.

Why it exists

PicButler started from a discomfort. Photo cleaners are one of the App Store's busiest categories, and many of its top apps charge $7.99 a week, hide the product behind aggressive paywalls and invent urgency. In 2025, security engineer Connor Tumbleson documented two of them charging his grandmother every week, with tracking services running even for paying users.

App Store reviews told the same story: people frustrated by surprise charges and bait-and-switch, who kept paying anyway, because a full iPhone is a real problem. If people pay even for apps that treat them badly, there is room for an honest one: photos analyzed on the device, no ads, a fair price, and an app that explains what it deletes and why. That is PicButler.

What it does

  • Finds your trips, nights out and holidays, month by month, and turns each one into a short chapter.
  • Groups duplicates and near-identical shots, keeps the best one and tells you why it won.
  • Lets you swipe through the rest, then saves what you keep as an album in Apple Photos.
  • Rediscover: every day, ten photos of one theme from across your years, free for everyone.
  • A Cleanup tab for similar photos, the largest videos, Live Photos and old screenshots.
  • No AI slop: PicButler never generates or retouches photos, and you make every final call.
