# Pricing: PicButler

PicButler is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later). Prices are in USD on the US App Store, as of October 29, 2026.
Purchases and cancellations go through the App Store.

## Free (after the trial)
- Price: $0
- Includes: curate one month of your photos, clear old screenshots, and use Rediscover every day

## PicButler Pro, monthly
- Price: $6.99 per month
- Free trial: none
- Includes: everything in PicButler

## PicButler Pro, annual
- Price: $29.99 per year, billed once
- Free trial: 7 days, for new subscribers
- Includes: everything in PicButler

Cancel anytime from the App Store. No weekly plans. No ads.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6760083866?pt=128539137&mt=8&ct=web
