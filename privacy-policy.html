Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 25, 2026

PicButler: Photo Cleaner ("PicButler", "we", "our") is designed with a simple principle: your photos never leave your device.

What we access

PicButler accesses your photo library through Apple's PhotoKit framework to:

  • Scan photos to detect duplicates and similar images
  • Analyze image quality (sharpness, lighting, resolution) to recommend the best version
  • Delete photos you choose to remove (with your explicit confirmation each time)

What we collect

Your photos stay on your device. All photo scanning, analysis, and scoring happens entirely on your iPhone. No photos, metadata, thumbnails, hashes, or analysis results are ever uploaded to any server.

Anonymous analytics

PicButler uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous, privacy-first analytics. This helps us understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

TelemetryDeck collects:

  • Anonymous event signals (e.g., "a scan was started") — with no way to link them to you
  • Basic device metadata (iOS version, device model, app version)

TelemetryDeck does not collect:

  • Your name, email, Apple ID, or any personal information
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or fingerprinting data
  • Your photos, photo metadata, or scan results
  • Your IP address (hashed on arrival and discarded)

All identifiers are double-hashed on device before transmission, making it impossible to identify individual users. TelemetryDeck is GDPR compliant by design. Learn more in TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

PicButler contains no advertising SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no crash reporting services.

No accounts

PicButler does not require login, registration, or any personal information. There is no account to create and no data to link to your identity.

Subscriptions

All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. PicButler never sees, stores, or has access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.

On-device storage

Scan results are stored locally on your device as a small data file. This file contains only photo identifiers and quality scores — not the photos themselves. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your control

  • You can revoke photo library access at any time in iOS Settings > PicButler
  • Photos are only deleted with your explicit confirmation through the iOS system prompt
  • Deleted photos go to your Recently Deleted album for 30 days before permanent removal
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data

Children's privacy

PicButler does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
